General Weather Advisory

🌿 Temperature Management

Above 35°C causes heat stress in most crops. Use shade nets, increase irrigation frequency, and apply mulch to keep soil temperature low.

💧 Water Conservation

Switch to drip irrigation to save 30-50% water. Irrigate in early morning or evening to reduce evaporation losses.

⚡ Monsoon Preparedness

Clean field drainage channels before monsoon. Avoid spraying pesticides before rain. Store harvested produce safely.
